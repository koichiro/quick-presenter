### Quick Presenter / Notes narration

# Let the notes
# do the talking

A PDF. A short script. One slide at a time.

<!--
Welcome to this short Quick Presenter demo. Each slide has a speaker note containing the words you are hearing. The demo reads those notes aloud, waits for the speech to finish, and then moves to the next slide.
-->

---

### 01 / A script behind each slide

## Short slides. Complete sentences.

- The audience sees the **key ideas**.
- Speaker notes carry the **spoken explanation**.
- This demo reads the notes **word for word**.

<!--
The audience sees only the key ideas on this slide. The full explanation lives in the speaker notes embedded in the PDF. This demo reads that explanation word for word. It does not invent an explanation or read the bullet points instead.
-->

---

### 02 / Let every sentence finish

## Speak. Finish. Advance.

1. Read the current slide's note.
2. Play the narration to completion.
3. Move forward **once**.

<!--
Notice that this slide stays on screen while the narration is playing. Only after the speech finishes does the demo ask Quick Presenter to move forward. If speech playback fails, the demo stops without sending a page change. Keep presentation control exclusive while the demo runs.
-->
